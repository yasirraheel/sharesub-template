Frequently asked questions

How does subscription sharing work?

Subscription sharing involves pooling a multi-account subscription among several people to split the cost. Rather than paying for a Netflix, Spotify, Disney+, or YouTube Premium subscription on your own, you share the available spots, and everyone pays only a fraction of the price.

On Sharesub, there are two ways to use the service. If you already have a shared subscription with open spots, you can offer them to others and get more value out of your subscription—there’s no cost to you in this case. If you’re looking to join a shared subscription, you can access dozens of services at a discounted price with just a few clicks.

Everything is automated and secure: payment, renewal, access management, and encrypted messaging between co-subscribers. Subscription sharing is legal as long as the service’s terms of use are followed. On average, our members save up to €480 per year by sharing their subscriptions.

Which subscriptions can I share on Sharesub?

Hundreds of services can be shared: video streaming (Netflix, Disney+, HBO Max, DAZN), music (Spotify, Deezer, YouTube Premium), as well as gaming, VPNs (NordVPN), online news, artificial intelligence tools (Google AI—similar to ChatGPT and Claude), and language learning (Duolingo). You can offer up unused slots on your multi-account subscriptions, or join an existing group to pay only a fraction of the cost.

How much does this service cost?

Subscription's owner
If you wish to share the cost of your subscription, the cost of the service is entirely free. There is no hidden fee: all the money you collect will be entirely delivered to your bank account. There are no fees for refunds either.
Subscription's participants
When you sign up for the subscription, you will pay the price of your spot in the subscription as determined by the owner, plus fixed and variable operating fees per payment. These fees are included in the listed prices. You will receive a full refund if the subscription does not work.

Is it legal to share a subscription?

Yes, it is legal to share a subscription as long as the terms of use are followed. Content and service providers themselves allow this sharing because they designed these plans for multiple users. In return, they can count on a larger user base that stays subscribed longer.

However, you must ensure that sharing complies with the terms and conditions of use so as not to violate them.

Sharesub, as a solution for managing recurring payments within a group, simply offers you the ability to manage reimbursements among yourselves—in a way that’s both simple and more secure.

Is Sharesub reliable and secure?

Yes. Sharesub is a French platform used by more than 900,000 members, with a rating of 4.8/5 on Google and Trustpilot. Payments are handled by a service provider authorized by the ACPR in compliance with the PSD2 directive: your banking information is never shared with other members. Every transaction is covered by our buyer protection program, which reimburses you if a subscription doesn’t work out. Communication takes place via an encrypted messaging system, and our team resolves disputes within 24 hours.

What if I change my mind? Can I cancel?

Yes, that's possible. You can choose to cancel at any time, subject to the terms and conditions. We don't recommend changing your mind too often, as this may disappoint the participants in your group.
If you are the subscription owner, canceling will notify your group and stop payments.
If you have joined a group, you are only committed for the current month. Cancel by the last day of the month at the latest to avoid paying for the next month.

Can I contact my co-subscribers?

Yes, each shared subscription benefits from group instant messaging, which allows you to chat with the owner and participants. This chat is encrypted and confidential.

Is the functioning of the subscription guaranteed?

Your subscription is guaranteed to work: once your payment card has been verified, you’ll gain access to the subscription and the information provided by the organizer. If that’s not enough or if an invitation is required, you have access to a secure messaging system to communicate with the organizer. Finally, you can file a complaint if your requests haven’t been resolved within 24 hours: our team will investigate and cancel your registration at no cost if necessary.

Do not purchase duplicate subscriptions while waiting for a response from an organizer: once you make a payment, you are committed to the current month if the subscription works properly.
